###
Hi, I'm [Faris](https://www.farisashai.me/)! (he/him) 👋
---

I’m an engineer who obsesses over the craft of building fast, scalable, thoughtfully-designed software. My background spans five years of broad engineering experience, but my specialty sits firmly in the frontend: modern frameworks (React, Solid, Svelte, Next.js, TypeScript), emerging primitives (container queries, data attributes, shadow DOM), and the architectural patterns that make large interfaces maintainable.

I care deeply about system design across every layer. Whether it’s reliability work, abstractions that keep codebases sane, or distributed systems built with Kafka, Redis/RabbitMQ, and SQL/NoSQL databases, I enjoy solving problems that demand clarity, structure, and long-term durability.

I’m also unusually passionate about performance. After two years working on iOS power optimization and activity scheduling—profiling render cycles, analyzing CPU/GPU/battery impact, and tuning on-device photo/Spotlight pipelines—I developed a habit of squeezing every inefficiency out of a system until it feels effortless.

At Formal, I lead the frontend function: UI/UX, component architecture, design systems, API contracts, backend workflows, and everything it takes to ship a polished product. I gravitate toward high-quality, detail-oriented work—micro-interactions, subtle animations, predictable state flows, and interfaces that feel alive.

Outside of work, I’m usually hunting for great coffee, matcha, tea, bakeries, or new food spots. I love music, sunsets, travel, skiing, billiards, and chess.
